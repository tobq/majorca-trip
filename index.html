How to book (in this order)

  1. Agree 1–5 above.
  2. Tobi books the room for 3 adults, with a note: "2 guests arriving ~21:00, 1 guest ~23:30".
  3. Straight after, Tobi + Nikhil book flights in one booking: Jet2 out, TUI back.
  4. Lewis books his own flights: Ryanair or Jet2 out, easyJet back.
  5. Nikhil and Lewis send Tobi their room share. Tourist tax (~€10 each) is paid at check-in.

Worth knowing

  • Spanish hotels register every guest's ID at check-in, so squeezing a third person into a room for two won't work (and would only save ~£56).
  • Booking flights and a room separately means no ATOL protection. Pay by credit card and get travel insurance.
  • Several rooms showed "2 left". Re-check prices with all seats together before paying.
  • We also priced Malta, Ibiza and Tenerife. All came out pricier, or meant much longer flights.
