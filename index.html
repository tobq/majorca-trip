How to book (in this order)

  1. Agree 1–3 above.
  2. Tobi books the place for 3 adults, with a note: "arriving ~21:00 and ~22:30, late check-out or bag storage on Sunday please".
  3. Straight after, Tobi + Nikhil book flights in one booking: Jet2 out, and Jet2 (evening) or TUI (morning) back.
  4. Lewis books his own flights: Jet2 out, and TUI (evening) or easyJet (morning) back.
  5. Nikhil and Lewis send Tobi their share of the room. Tourist tax (~€10 each) is paid at check-in.

Worth knowing

  • Spanish hotels register every guest's ID at check-in, so squeezing a third person into a room for two won't work.
  • Booking flights and the room separately means no ATOL protection. Pay by credit card and get travel insurance.
  • Several rooms showed "2 left". Re-check prices with all seats together before paying.
  • We also priced Malta, Ibiza and Tenerife. All came out pricier, or meant much longer flights.
